# 2026-09-25 — TKT-251 (books "stopped") and TKT-242 ("solved correctly, marked wrong")

## TKT-251 — user-books pipeline

**What the coach saw (18 Sep 21:23 IST):** 7 of 16 books at "0 positions · 0%", one at 79%.

**What was true:** 11 PDFs uploaded 13:32–14:05 UTC. Before 14:22 UTC the vision service
rendered every book concurrently (load 24 on 8 cores — the queue fix `cb8435b`/`c856a19`
landed that afternoon); after it, one at a time. The ticket was filed mid-queue; the last
book finished 17:46 UTC (23:16 IST). Nothing was broken — but nothing said "queued" either,
and the design had a real hole:

- `book_ingest.py` keeps the queue in memory (`_WAITING`/`_ACTIVE`). Restart the service
  (deploy, crash, reboot) and status.json keeps saying queued/rendering for ever; nothing
  re-reads those books. `/book/ingest` trusted the file, so re-uploading could not restart
  them either.
- The API's "already have this file" path only linked the coach; never re-kicked.
- The shelf chip showed `0%` for `state=queued` and for `state=error`.

**Fix (7026fed):**
- vision service (`/opt/chessguru-vision`, live; mirrored into `v2/vision-service/`):
  `_OWNED` set + `owns()`/`stranded()`; `_book_classify`/`_book_detect` hoisted out of the
  endpoint; `_book_sweeper` thread 30 s after boot and every 300 s re-`start()`s stranded
  books; the endpoint refuses only when `owns()`. Env knobs `BOOK_SWEEP`, `BOOK_SWEEP_FIRST_S`,
  `BOOK_SWEEP_EVERY_S`.
- API `user-books.controller.ts`: stored copy with `state !== "done"` → POST `/book/ingest`.
- Web `MyBooks.tsx`: chips `queued` / `failed` / `N%` / `ready`; hint line for failed.

**Verified:** staged `zz-sweep-test` (1-page PDF, `status.json {state:queued}`) → restarted
`chessguru-ultra-vision` → adopted and `done` in 8.3 s. `stranded()` on the real store → `[]`.
All 16 gunachess books `state=done` with diagrams.json populated. Test dirs removed.

**Note:** the repo copy of `vision-service/*.py` had drifted behind `/opt` since 19 Sep; edit
`/opt` (owned by `ubuntu`, `sudo -n cp`), then copy back to the repo.

## TKT-242 — puzzle #MRZuh

FEN after 1.Kxh2: `5rk1/2Q2p2/3P4/p5q1/2B3p1/P5n1/1P3P1K/R4R2 b`. Student: Qh5+. Stockfish 18
depth 30: Qh4+ mate in 3 (Kg2 Qh3+ Kg1 Qh1#); Qh5+ **Kxg3!** and White is +7.7 (queen on h4
guards the g3 knight, on h5 it does not). The checker (`usePuzzleGame.ts`: exact line or
immediate mate) was right.

**Fix (e65538c):** `PuzzleRefuteService` + `GET /api/puzzles/:id/refute?wrong=<uci>` —
replays the stored line to the solver's ply, plays the wrong move, one shared Stockfish
(12 s budget, serialised, idle-stopped after 5 min, results cached). `Puzzles.tsx` shows
one sentence under "You played" (`refuteText`). Live: `Kxg3`, cp −525, 2.3 s cold / 72 ms cached.
